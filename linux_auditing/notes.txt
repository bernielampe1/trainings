* Driver auditing section
    talk about DAC and MAC (selinux) and how it's implemented
    identify what is available /dev/ and /proc/ and to whom
    identify fops for each available thing
    focus on things that are implemented by vendors or third party (lower hanging fruit)
    make sure you understand what open and release are doing and are matched up
    focus on mmap and that they check parameters correctly
    focus on extensible drivers such as ion
    focus on things in the driver which are overrode in higher level like /proc level
